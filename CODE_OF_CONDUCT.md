# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Focus feedback on the work, assume good intent, and avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or disclosure of another person's private information.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be edited, removed, or rejected, and contributors may be temporarily or permanently restricted from project spaces. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact options on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential where practical.

This policy applies in project repositories, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the project.
